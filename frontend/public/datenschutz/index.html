WhatsApp messages

Google API Services – Limited Use

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is not used to train generalised AI models.

  • Hosting: servers in Germany (EU); encrypted backups in the EU
  • AI features: OpenAI and Google (voice assistant).   • Synced Gmail data: while the Gmail connection or company account remains active — or until you delete it, whichever occurs first
  • Encrypted backups are deleted automatically after 30 days at the latest
  • OAuth tokens and passwords are stored encrypted or hashed
  • Transmission exclusively over TLS (HTTPS)
  • Assistant histories: automatic deletion after 90 days; deleted documents are permanently removed after 30 days

6. Your rights — disconnect & delete data

You have the right to access, rectification, deletion and data portability. Directly in the app you can at any time:

  • Disconnect Gmail: Settings → Connections → “Disconnect” — we also revoke the access with Google (token revocation)
  • Delete Gmail data: Settings → Connections → “Delete Gmail data” — removes all synced emails, including translations and AI summaries, completely from our active systems. Email attachments are not stored permanently (they are loaded from Gmail on demand). Residual copies in encrypted backups are deleted automatically after 30 days at the latest
  • Disconnect WhatsApp: Settings → Connections → number “Log out”
  • Delete WhatsApp data: Settings → Connections → number “Remove” — deletes the connection and all synced chats, messages and media files completely from our servers

You can also revoke access at any time in your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. For anything else: info@future-media.ch.
